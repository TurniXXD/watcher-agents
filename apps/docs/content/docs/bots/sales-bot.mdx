## Overview

Sales Bot discovers Czech businesses through ARES and Geoapify, optionally uses
Google Places fallback, crawls public company websites, stores evidence, scores
leads, prepares grounded drafts, and integrates with Quickly and Twenty when
configured. Personal contacts and business-card reading are also available in
the separate Networking Bot. Sales keeps the same commands for backward
compatibility; event discovery and event follow-ups belong only to Networking.

Use `/start`, `/help`, or `/sales_help` for the current guide. Sales commands
include `/sales_status`, `/sales_campaigns`, `/sales_prospects`,
`/sales_search`, `/sales_searches`, `/sales_search_stop`, `/sales_find`,
`/sales_calls`, `/sales_leads`, `/sales_lead`, `/sales_approve`,
`/sales_reject`, and `/sales_run`. Sales intelligence adds `/link_to_lead URL`
(with `/lead URL` as an alias),
`/lead_analysis ID`, `/lead_refresh ID`, `/leads_today`, `/leads_next`, and
`/leads_stats`. It caches sourced research for 14 days. On working days at 09:00
Europe/Prague the bot reserves up to five eligible uncontacted leads and queues
a summary plus detailed reports. A suggestion never marks a lead contacted and
never sends outreach; Saturday and Sunday produce no batch.

The backward-compatible contact commands continue to use the `/network_`
prefix and the same preview-and-confirm business-card workflow as Networking
Bot.

Discovery maps a Czech or English business description to validated Geoapify and
CZ-NACE categories, queries providers, enriches public sites, records evidence,
and scores leads. Immediate and periodic search results use compact lead blocks
instead of fixed-width tables; valid public company and provider-detail HTTP(S)
URLs are clickable. Periodic search stores its cursor and emits only new contacts.
Lead research and call lists do not send email.

`/link_to_lead URL` creates or refreshes the deduplicated internal research
record, sends its sourced intelligence report to Telegram, and presents Save
and Cancel buttons. Save is valid for 15 minutes and idempotently upserts the
Twenty Company plus a linked research Note. Cancel, expiry, a bot restart, or a
confirmation from a different operator writes nothing to Twenty.

`/sales_prospects <business type> | <location> | [limit]` performs a separate
campaign-free CRM import. It defaults to 10 results, accepts 1–20, audits public
websites for an email and explicit `tel:` link, and upserts a Twenty Company plus
a linked Note containing the contact values and source URLs. PostgreSQL records
provider identity, domain, IČO, contact evidence, and Twenty IDs so subsequent
runs skip a completed prospect. The command does not create a campaign, draft,
approval, or Quickly enrollment.

Quickly enrollment fails closed unless a qualified approved lead has explicit
`RECIPIENT_OPT_IN` or `EXISTING_CUSTOMER` evidence, no suppression, an enabled
campaign, and available capacity. Public contact data or Telegram approval alone
is not consent.

The Twenty Sales App also forwards a new `Person` to Sales Bot when Twenty marks
its creator as `EMAIL`, or when an external contact-form integration explicitly
sets `leadSource=CONTACT_FORM` (or a documented contact-form alias). Sales Bot
queues an idempotent Telegram notification for every authorized operator.
Manual records and ordinary Sales imports through ARES, Geoapify, Google Places,
or `/sales_prospects` do not produce this notification.

### Dedicated inbound lead mailbox

An optional TLS IMAP connector polls a dedicated lead mailbox in read-only mode.
It accepts the visitor's external `Reply-To` from contact-form envelopes,
ignores and never stores attachments, bounds message size and count, and never changes mailbox
state or sends a reply. Automated/bulk/transactional messages are rejected by
deterministic rules. Other mail receives a structured, schema-validated Ollama
classification when Ollama is configured, with deterministic fallback.

Relevant messages and uncertain human enquiries are idempotently upserted as a
Twenty Person with a linked analysis Note and the descriptive source marker
`MAILBOX_EMAIL` or `MAILBOX_CONTACT_FORM`. Those markers intentionally do not
match the Twenty app's contact-form aliases, because this mailbox pipeline owns
the single durable Telegram notification. Telegram receives only the compact
classification, source, intent, requested services, factual summary, and CRM
link. Message-ID or a stable fallback hash is persisted with the durable
outbox, so repeated lookback polls and restarts do not duplicate the lead or
alert.

## Execution and scheduling

```bash
pnpm --filter @watcher/sales-bot dev
pnpm --filter @watcher/sales-bot typecheck
pnpm exec vitest run apps/sales-bot/src/__tests__
```

Periodic work defaults to 30-minute intervals; inbound mailbox polling defaults
to 5 minutes; follow-up reporting defaults to
20:00 in `Europe/Prague`. Manual and scheduled discovery use the same persisted
search and lead pipeline. The campaign-free prospect import is one-shot and must
be invoked explicitly. Twenty inbound-lead alerts are event driven rather than
scheduled; their PostgreSQL outbox retries bounded Telegram failures across
restarts.

## Integrations

| Source/service                       | Role                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ARES                                 | Official Czech company identity, registered address, IČO, and CZ-NACE evidence. |
| Geoapify/OpenStreetMap               | Primary place discovery and available public contact metadata.                  |
| Google Places                        | Optional fallback when primary sources lack enough contacts.                    |
| Public company websites              | Contact-page, explicit e-mail, and `tel:` evidence with source URLs.            |
| Inbound TLS IMAP mailbox             | Read-only contact-form/e-mail ingestion, analysis, and stable message identity. |
| Twenty CRM                           | Idempotent Company/Person/Note state plus filtered inbound alerts.              |
| Quickly                              | Explicitly authorized campaign sequences after every eligibility gate passes.   |
| Ollama                               | Optional category mapping and schema-validated draft generation.                |
| Google Sheets/contact-network        | Backward-compatible personal-contact workflow shared with Networking Bot.       |
| Telegram, Sales REST API, PostgreSQL | Private control surfaces and authoritative workflow state.                      |

## Troubleshooting

- An empty call list means no analyzed lead currently has a public phone; run
  search/discovery and analysis, then inspect evidence.
- `/sales_prospects` imports only candidates with a website. A result without an
  official website is counted as found but is not written to Twenty.
- Category mapping prints the Geoapify/CZ-NACE categories actually used. Supply
  `geo:` or `nace:` for an explicit override.
- Quickly refusal is expected when consent, suppression, campaign, or capacity
  requirements are not satisfied.
- If an inbound Person produces no alert, verify the app logic function is
  applied, its secret token matches `SALES_WEBHOOK_TOKEN`, and the record is
  either created by the `EMAIL` actor or explicitly tagged as a contact form.
