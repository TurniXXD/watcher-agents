## Failure and quota policy

Provider-specific formats are normalized inside their adapters. Timeouts,
rate-limit backoff, source concurrency, and caches are bounded by configuration.
A failing source is recorded and does not become an empty-success result.
Scraping that bypasses CAPTCHA, authentication, or anti-bot controls is not a
fallback.

Socials retries transient failures and HTTP 429 responses with bounded backoff.
RSS/Atom requests validate DNS targets and every redirect against private and
special-purpose networks, and reuse `ETag`/`Last-Modified` validators. Generic
social/news sources remain `GLOBAL` unless a provider supplies defensible
regional evidence.

## Optional providers

An optional key activates only its owning adapter. For example, Stocks core
thesis coverage does not require paid options data; Sales can research without
Quickly or Twenty; Socials accepts authorized manual trend exports when broad
official discovery APIs are unavailable.
